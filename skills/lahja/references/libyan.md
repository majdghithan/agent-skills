# Libyan Arabic (ليبي) - writing guide

**Coverage:** Tripoli / western Libyan, the media standard, with Benghazi / eastern notes. Every form below is cited to the sources at the end; anything the sources didn't settle is listed in the last section and must not be generated.

**Evidence tags:** unmarked = published source. `[corpus]` = counted in a 4,061-segment corpus of spontaneous Libyan speech in Arabic script, so it is attested usage rather than a grammarian's claim. Eastern forms are one notch less certain throughout, because the eastern reference grammars are offline.

## 0. Libyan is not Tunisian and not Egyptian. Eight divergences

This is the whole reason the guide exists. An agent that reaches for a neighbour gets these wrong:

| Point | Neighbour | Libyan |
|---|---|---|
| Habitual "I say" | Egyptian بقول, Moroccan كنقول | **بare imperfect: نقول.** Libyan has **no habitual preverb at all**. [Sellami] |
| بـ + verb | Egyptian habitual | **intention future**: بنقول = "I'm going to say", never "I say". [Pereira] [Algryani] |
| باش | Tunisian and Algerian future ("will") | **purpose only**: باش نقول لهم = "so that I tell them". [corpus] |
| "what" | Egyptian إيه, Moroccan آش | **شن** (also شنو) [corpus] |
| "very / a lot" | Tunisian برشا, Algerian ياسر, Egyptian أوي | **هلبا** (west), **واجد** (east) [ArXivLid] |
| "now" | Moroccan دابا, Egyptian دلوقتي, Levantine هلق | **توّا** [corpus] |
| Verbal ـش | obligatory in Egyptian and Moroccan | **optional in eastern Libyan**: ما كتب (ش) [Owens via Algryani] |
| "of" | Moroccan ديال, Egyptian بتاع | **نتاع** / **متاع** [corpus] |

## 1. Negation

- **Verbal negation = ما...ش** around the verb, past and present: علي ما كتبش الرسالة، ما نعرفش، ما نقدرش، ما عندهاش. [Algryani] [corpus]
- **Eastern Libyan may drop the ـش**: ما كتب. Don't do this in a Tripoli text. [Algryani citing Owens]
- **Nominal negation = مش**, standing between subject and predicate: عمر مش هنا، مش علي اللي جا. Write مش, not موش (zero corpus hits). [Algryani] [corpus]
- **Negated future uses مش, not ما...ش**: علي مش حيسافر بكرة. This is the most-missed rule in the dialect. [Algryani]
- **Pronoun copula negation = ما + pronoun + ش**: ماهوش، ماهيش، ماهمش، مانيش. نادية ماهيش هنا. [corpus]
- **Existential**: positive في / فيه; negative **ما فيش** (also written مافيش). Past: ما كانش، ما كانتش، ما كانوش. [corpus]
- **Prohibition = ما + 2nd-person imperfect + ش** (not the imperative stem): ما تمشيش، ما توقفش، ما تتكلمش. [corpus]
- **نبي "want" negates the same way**: ما نبيش، ما نبوش، مابيش. [corpus]
- **Contrast also takes مش**: عمر مشى للسينما، مش للجامعة. [Algryani]

## 2. Future: four markers, not one

Libyan has a layered future. Pick by intent. [Pereira] [Algryani] [corpus]

| Marker | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **حـ / حا‍ـ** | certainty, the neutral future; commoner among younger educated speakers | حيكون، حتوصل، حنشوفو |
| **بـ** | intention | بنقول، بندير، بنشوفك غدوة |
| **تو‍ـ / توّ‍ـ** | commitment or threat, **1st person with a human subject only** | تونورّيك، توندوّرلك |
| **راح + imperfect** | motion-participle future, less frequent | راح نسافر |

- The bare imperfect also carries future in subordinate and hypothetical clauses. [Pereira]
- **باش is not a future marker in Libyan.** It means "so that". [corpus]
- **Not sourced:** ني as a future marker. It appears in no source and nowhere in the corpus.

## 3. Aspect

- **Bare imperfect = habitual or generic**: ياخدوا السحلب ويطحنوه في طاحونة. Libyan has **no** Egyptian بـ- and **no** Moroccan كـ- habitual. [Sellami]
- **The progressive must be marked.** Two ways: [Sellami] [Pereira]
  - **قاعد / گاعد + imperfect**, agreeing (قاعد / قاعدة / قاعدين): الشغل اللي قاعد يحدث، أنا گاعدة نطيّب في مكرونة. Here قاعد does **not** mean "sitting".
  - **في between verb and object**: نشرب في شاهي = "I'm drinking tea". This في is an aspect marker, not the preposition.
- **راهو / راهي / راني / راهم** is a real presentative-assertive particle in Libyan speech: هذا سوق راهو. Treat it as emphasis, not as the progressive marker. `[corpus]`
- **Active participle = imminent action**: ماشية "I'm off". [Pereira]
- **Bare imperfect after modals**, no linker: نبي نجي، يقدر يكتب رسالة، ضروري علي يجي. [Algryani]
- **Past habitual and past progressive are both كان + imperfect**: علي كان يدخّن. [Algryani]

## 4. Demonstratives and question words

- **Near**: هذا / هاذا (m), هذي / هاذي (f), هذو / هذوما / هذوم (pl). **Far**: هذاك / هذيك، plural هذين / هذينا. Emphatic: هذايا، هذيا. [Pereira] [corpus]
- Write **هذي**, not the MSA هذه, in dialect text. [corpus]
- **Question words**: [corpus] [Algryani] [ArXivLid]

| Meaning | Libyan |
|---|---|
| what | **شن**, also شنو (m) / شني (f, western) |
| who | من / منو / مني |
| where | وين، لوين ("to where")، منين ("from where") |
| why | علاش (west) / ليش (east and centre) |
| how | كيف (**never كيفاش**) |
| when | امتى (**never متين**) |
| how much | قداش / گداش, also كم |
| which | أي / أيّا + noun |

- **Relative = اللي**, overwhelmingly (656 corpus hits vs 5 for الذي). [corpus]

## 5. Pronouns: Libyan is Maghrebi here

- **The n- pattern applies**: **نكتب = "I write"**, **نكتبوا = "we write"**. An agent that writes أكتب or Egyptian بكتب for "I write" is instantly not Libyan. This is the single most important form in the guide. [LibyaWiki] [Algryani] [corpus]
- **Independent pronouns**: اني (the characteristic Libyan "I", commoner than أنا), انت، انتي، هو، هي، **احنا** (Tripoli also احني; eastern and central نحنا)، انتوا، هما. Avoid انتم, which is an MSA leak. [Algryani] [corpus]
- **Possessive particle: نتاع** (commonest) or **متاع**, with a feminine-agreeing متاعت- before a feminine possessor: الإنجازات نتاعنا، الشنطة متاعتها. حق exists but is marginal, and ديال / تاع are not Libyan. [corpus] [ArWikiLibya]

## 6. Spelling habits

- **ق is pronounced [g] but written ق** in ordinary text. گ is also genuine Libyan practice when someone is writing pronunciation deliberately (گالوا، حايگون), but the corpus is 3,722 ق against 687 گ. Default to ق and stay consistent. [LibyaWiki] [corpus]
- **ج is generally [ʒ]**; the western sibilant-harmony spellings (زوز for جوز) are speech, not standard writing. [ArXivLid]
- **Interdentals split west/east**: Tripoli merges ث ذ ظ into stops, Benghazi keeps them. **Spelling does not follow the merger**: write هذا، هذي، كذاب with their etymological letters even for Tripoli. [LibyaWiki] [EastLibyaWiki] [corpus]
- **Final ة** stays ة. Converting it to ه is NLP preprocessing, not Libyan writing. [ArXivLid]
- **Write Arabic script.** The corpus has zero Latin-script tokens. [corpus]
- **Not sourced:** any Libya-specific Arabizi convention, including how [g] gets romanized.

## 8. Foreign tech terms: Italian legacy, English present

- **Italian is the historical layer, English is the live one.** Libya is the only Maghreb country whose European contact language was Italian rather than French. Italian loans are about 93% nouns and mostly material vocabulary: كمبيو (gearbox)، فوركيتة (fork)، مرشابيدي (sidewalk). Few Libyans under about 60 speak Italian now. [Benkato]
- **Loan verbs exist but the class is closed**: فريز "to break down", فران "to brake". Don't coin new ones. [Benkato]
- **Turkish layer**, mostly domestic nouns plus دغري "straight ahead" and بلكي "maybe". [Benkato]
- **English tech terms are written in ARABIC script** in real Libyan text: اوكي، ميديا، تيم، سيستم، بوست، شير، بروجكت، كلاس، ايميل، كمبيوتر، انترنت. The corpus has **zero** Latin-script tokens. [corpus]
- **This is the opposite of the Levantine and Gulf default.** In a Libyan text, transliterate the tech term instead of leaving it in Latin, unless the user's own text uses Latin script.

## 9. Worked rewrites (MSA -> Tripoli)

Grammar comes from the sections above; ordinary content words are not individually sourced.

**1. Habitual vs intention** (the بـ trap)
- MSA: أكتب التقارير كل أسبوع، وسوف أرسل التقرير اليوم.
- Libyan: نكتب التقارير كل أسبوع، وبنبعت التقرير اليوم.
- Bare نكتب for the habit, بـ for the intention. Egyptian بكتب would be wrong for the first.

**2. Negated future** (the مش rule)
- MSA: لن نطلق التحديث اليوم، لا يوجد وقت.
- Libyan: مش حنطلقو التحديث اليوم، ما فيش وقت.
- Negated future takes مش, not ما...ش.

**3. Progressive + what** (obligatory progressive marking)
- MSA: ماذا تفعل الآن؟
- Libyan: شن قاعد تدير توّا؟
- Bare تدير would read as habitual, so قاعد is required.

**4. Want + can't** (نبي, n- prefix, tech term in Arabic script)
- MSA: أريد أن أفتح البرنامج لكنني لا أستطيع.
- Libyan: نبي نفتح البرنامج، بس ما نقدرش.

## 10. Deliberately NOT included

Not attested, or actively contradicted, in the sources checked: **متين** for "when" (the corpus hit is the adjective "solid"; use امتى); **ني** as a future marker; **كيفاش** and **وقتاش** (Tunisian, Algerian, Moroccan); **شكون** (Maghrebi, use من / منو); **هاذوكم**; **موش** (write مش); **فين** (Egyptianism); بـ as a habitual marker; باش as a plain future; newly coined Italian or English loan verbs; Libya-specific Arabizi rules.
