# Egyptian Arabic (مصري) - writing guide

**Coverage:** Cairene, the Egyptian standard in media and online. Alexandria and Upper Egypt differ and are not covered. Every form below is cited to the sources at the end; anything the sources didn't settle is marked and must not be generated.


**Confidence tags:** unmarked = sourced from Wikipedia, CODA, corpus papers, or academic articles. `(learner)` = only a learner or reference site (Lisaan Masry, Lingualism, Wiktionary). Forms we could not source are not listed.

## 1. Negation

- **ما...ش wraps the verb**, past and present, including attached objects: ماكتبش، مابيكتبش، مابحبش. Write -ش, not the old-film -شي. [EgyWiki] [LM]
- **ما...ش is the default with بـ-verbs.** مش + بـ-verb (مش بحب) is less common and carries a special meaning: contrast, "don't you dare", or negating an ongoing action: مش بكلمك على الفلوس، بكلمك عنك. [LM] [Wilmsen]
- **مش with everything that isn't a conjugated verb**: nouns, adjectives, places, participles, عايز: هي مش هنا، أنا مش مصري، مش عارف، مش عايز. [NegWiki] [LM]
- **Negated future: مش + هـ**: مش هشتري الكتاب، مش هيكتب. [EgyWiki] [LM]
- **مش + past verb as a question** = "didn't he?": مش كتب؟ [EgyWiki]
- **Existential**: مافيش (also spelled مفيش). **Possession**: ماعنديش / معنديش. [LM] [CODA]
- **Prohibition**: ماتنساش، ماتمشيش. [LM] [ArzWikiStyle]
- **Don't use** مهوش / مانيش; they are not attested for Cairo (Tunisian and Levantine data only). Use مش هو / مش أنا. [Wilmsen]
- **ما attached or separate** (ماكتبش vs ما كتبش): both appear. Pick one per text. [Lingualism] [CODA]

## 2. Future

- **هـ prefix**: هروح، هتخلص إمتى؟، هنقفل. Most people now write هـ because that is how Cairo pronounces it; حـ (حروح) is older/Alexandrian and still seen. **Use هـ.** [Lingualism] [LM] [CODA]
- **Never** سـ / سوف, and never the Levantine رح.

## 3. Aspect and mood

- **بـ prefix** = present, habitual, and in progress: بيكتب، بتكتب، بنكتب. 1sg is written بكتب or باكتب; pick one. [EgyWiki] [LM]
- **No عم.** Egyptian marks "right now" with plain بـ, with a participle, or optionally قاعد: بيدرس = "studies / is studying"; قاعد بيدرس. [Ouali21] [LM]
- **عمّال + بـ-verb** = "keeps on doing it", often with annoyance: عمّال بيلعب. [Ouali21]
- **Participles** carry present or ongoing meaning: أنا رايح النادي (on my way now) vs بروح النادي (I go regularly); فاهم، نايمة، عامل إيه. [DesertSky] [LM]
- **Bare verb, no أنْ**, after لازم، ممكن، عايز: لازم أكتب، ممكن تقولي، عايز ياكل. [LM] [Wikt]

## 4. Demonstratives and question words

- **ده / دي / دول come AFTER the noun**: الكتاب ده، الـ tasks دي. Spelling: CODA picks ده over دا; كده is more common than كدا. [VarWiki] [LM] [CODA]
- **Question words usually go at the end**: رايح فين؟، راح مصر إمتى؟، أشحن التليفون ده إزاي؟، إنت منين؟. Question-first also occurs (مين راح مصر؟), but the end position is what makes text sound Egyptian. [EgyWiki] [LM]
- **List**: إيه، إمتى، فين، منين، إزاي، ليه، مين، كام، بكام، قد إيه، أنهي. [LM]
- **كام**: كام + singular asks "how many" (عندك كام ولد؟); noun + كام asks "what number" (الساعة كام؟). [LM]
- **Relative**: اللي. **That**: إنّ: أفتكر إنك غلطان (this is Egyptian, not a leak). [LM]
- **Because / so that**: عشان / علشان (with a subjunctive it means "so that"). [Wikt-ashan]
- **Possession with بتاع** (m) / بتاعة (f) / بتوع (pl), after the noun: الكتاب بتاع أحمد، القميص ده بتاعك. Natural for "the X of Y" where MSA would use a construct. [Wikt-betaa]
- **Not verified yet:** لمّا for "when". Avoid it until it is sourced.

## 5. Pronouns

أنا، إحنا، إنتَ، إنتِ، إنتو، هو، هي، همّ. Suffixes: ـك (بيتك، أبوكي after a vowel)، ـكو (CODA spelling, no alif)، ـهم. [EgyWiki] [CODA] [LM]

## 6. Spelling habits

Everyday Egyptian writing and CODA disagree. **Pick one convention for the whole text.** For product or brand copy, the default below avoids ambiguity while still looking Egyptian.

| Point | Common Egyptian habit | CODA | lahja default |
|---|---|---|---|
| ق (said ʔ) | ق | ق | **ق** (قال, not أال) |
| ج (said g) | ج | ج | **ج** |
| ث / ذ said t / d | ت / د: كتير، تاني، خد | etymological: كثير | **follow pronunciation**: كتير، تاني |
| ث / ذ said s / z | keep ث / ذ: ثانية، ذكي | keep | **keep** |
| Final ي | often ى (فى، على/علي confusion) | ي dotted | **dotted ي** (في، علي vs على distinct) |
| Final ة | often ه (حريه) | ة | **ة** |
| Plural verb alif | بيكتبو / بيكتبوا | بيكتبوا | **بيكتبوا** |
| Hamza on alif | often dropped (انا، اول) | written | writer's choice, consistent |

Sources: [Lingualism] [CODA] [ArzWikiStyle] [EgyWiki].

- **Tanween:** gone as grammar; it survives only on frozen adverbs (أحياناً، عملياً). Don't add it anywhere else. [CODA] [Lingualism]

## 8. English tech terms

- **Heavy code-switching is normal** among educated Egyptians. In a technical-field interview corpus, 37% of words were English and 80% of sentences mixed. [Hamed18] [ArzEn]
- **English verbs take Arabic prefixes** in the present: بيـask، بتـhandle. 80% of inserted English verbs took Arabic tense/person prefixes; the past tense usually doesn't integrate. [Kniaz21] [ArzEnST]
- **Arabic affixes on English nouns**: الـ tasks دي. Almost half of mixed sentences put Arabic prefixes or suffixes on English words. [ArzEnST]
- **In writing, people mostly switch without changing script**: English stays Latin. Common loanwords appear in both scripts (موبايل / mobile). [ArzEnST]
- **Arabized verb attested**: سيّف (سييڤ الصفحه). [ArzWikiStyle]
- **Not verified for Egyptian:** كنسل (a dictionary gives لغى for "cancel"), شيّر. Don't use them in an Egyptian text.
- **Publishing default:** keep the English word whole in Latin script, and put the Arabic prefix before it with a tatweel and a space where needed (الـ tasks). Glued forms like بتـhandle are real speech but hard to read in published copy; use them only in chat-style text.

## 9. Worked rewrites (MSA -> Cairene)

These use only the grammar forms above; common content words (شغّال، حاجة، قفل) are not individually sourced.

**1. Debugging** (negation, question word at the end)
- MSA: لا أعرف لماذا لا يعمل الـ build، لم أغيّر أي شيء.
- Egy: مش عارف الـ build مش شغّال ليه، ماغيّرتش حاجة.

**2. Announcement** (سوف, لن)
- MSA: سوف ننقل المشروع من MySQL إلى PostgreSQL، ولن نغلق الموقع.
- Egy: هننقل المشروع من MySQL لـ PostgreSQL، ومش هنقفل الموقع.

**3. Team chat** (يريد, الآن, أين)
- MSA: ماذا تريد أن تفعل الآن؟ أين الملف؟
- Egy: عايز تعمل إيه دلوقتي؟ الملف فين؟

**4. Tech mixing** (demonstrative after the noun, English inline)
- MSA: كيف ستتعامل مع هذه الـ tasks؟ أنا أعمل على الـ feature الآن.
- Egy: هتعمل إيه في الـ tasks دي؟ أنا بشتغل على الـ feature دلوقتي.
