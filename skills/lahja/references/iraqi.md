# Iraqi Arabic (عراقي) - writing guide

**Coverage:** Baghdadi Muslim Arabic (the **gilit** dialect), the media and online standard, with notes for southern (Basra, Amarah, Nasiriyah) and Mosuli/northern qeltu, which differ in ways that break a text if blended. Every form below is cited to the sources at the end; anything the sources didn't settle is listed in the last section and must not be generated.


**Confidence tags:** unmarked = Alkalesi's Georgetown textbook, Albuarabi's dissertation on Iraqi negation, or Wikipedia citing specialists. `(weak)` = Wiktionary or native-answer threads only. Forms we could not source are left out, and section 10 lists what was deliberately dropped.

## 0. Sub-dialect table

| Feature | Baghdadi gilit (default) | Southern (Basra, Nasiriyah) | Mosuli qeltu |
|---|---|---|---|
| "not" + verb | ما | **ما...ش** and مش | ما |
| In progress | دا- | گا- | كِ- / قِ- / عِ- |
| ق | said g, written گ | g | stays q (اقول) |
| ك before front vowels | said ch, written چ | چ | stays ك |
| ر | r | r | merges toward غ (راح -> ʁaħ) |

Blending these is the fastest way to sound fake. Pick Baghdadi unless the user says otherwise. [Albuarabi] [NorthMeso] [SouthMeso]

## 1. Negation

- **ما + verb** (never with an imperative): ما يعرف، ما يريد، ما رحت. [Alkalesi]
- **مو + nouns, adjectives, adverbs** (nominal predicates only): علي مو طالب، مو مشكلة. [Alkalesi] [Wikt-mu]
- **أكو / ماكو** = "there is" / "there isn't": ماكو أكل بالثلاجة. **ماكو** also works as a prohibition before a verb: ماكو تخبرني كل نص ساعة. [Wikt-aku] [Albuarabi]
- **Possession**: ما عندي، ما معك. [Alkalesi]
- **Prohibition**: لا + imperfect: لا تسافر، لا تسافرين، لا تسافرون. [Alkalesi]
- **ولا** links further negated items: ما أجي ولا أروح. [Alkalesi]
- **Negation sits outside the aspect marker**: ما دا يدرس, not ~~دا ما يدرس~~. [Albuarabi]
- **With a demonstrative**: مو goes **before** it when the noun is definite (مو هالبيت), **after** it when indefinite (هذاك مو بيت). [Alkalesi]
- **No ...ش in Baghdadi**, but **southern Iraqi does use ما...ش and مش**. Don't write ما أعرفش in a Baghdadi text, and don't tell a Basrawi it isn't Iraqi. [Albuarabi]

## 2. Future

- **رح- or حـ- + imperfect**: رح أدرس، رح يقرا كتاب، حـ- often feels more immediate. راح is a variant; Alkalesi recommends the short رح. [Alkalesi]
- Before a two-consonant start, a helping vowel appears: رح إنسافر. [Alkalesi]
- **راح also works as a standalone auxiliary**: أحمد ما راح يروح للجامعة. [Albuarabi] [Wikt-rah]
- **Negated future**: ما راح + verb. [Albuarabi]
- **No بـ future.** Iraqi بـ / بي- is the preposition "in, with", so a Gulf-style بروح collides with it. [Alkalesi] [Persson]

## 3. Aspect - the single most important Iraqi rule

- **Bare imperfect = habitual**, not "right now": نشرب چاي = we drink tea. [Alkalesi]
- **دا- + imperfect = happening now**: دا نشرب چاي = we are drinking tea. More pairs: آكل خبز vs دا آكل خبز; وين تروح كل يوم؟ vs وين دا تروح اليوم؟ [Alkalesi]
- **An agent that writes the bare verb for "right now" sounds wrong in Iraqi.** This is the highest-value rule in this guide.
- **But دا- marks an action already under way, not a future one.** With رح / حـ, or after a modal, use the bare imperfect even when the sentence says "now": رح أشوفه هسّه, **not** ~~دا أشوفه هسّه~~. هسّه in a future sentence does not license دا-.
- **گاعد + imperfect** is a fuller progressive (more southern/Najafi in the sourced data); دا- is the Baghdadi default. [Albuarabi]
- **Regional forms of the same marker**: Baghdadi دا-، Najafi/southern گا-، Mosuli كِ-/قِ-/عِ-. [Albuarabi]
- **No بـ indicative prefix** anywhere in Iraqi. Don't import Levantine بكتب. [Alkalesi] [Persson]
- **Bare imperfect after modals**: أريد أروح، لازم أشوف، أگدر أصرّف. Note أريد is genuinely Iraqi here, not a formal-Arabic leak. [Alkalesi]

## 4. Demonstratives and question words

- **Near**: هاذا m، هاذي f (colloquial written هاي)، هذولا pl. **Far**: هذاك، هذيچ، هذولاك. [Alkalesi] [Wikt-hay]
- **هـ- before a definite noun is the more common usage**, assimilating to sun letters: هالولد، هالبيت، هالسيارة، هالطالبة، and with an English noun هالـ query. [Alkalesi]
- **Question words**: شنو (also the proclitic شـ-: شسمك؟ شدا تسوّي؟)، وين، منين/إمنين، ليش (variants الويش، لويش)، شلون، شوكت، منو، شگد، بيش، چم/كم، أي / يا / ياهو. [Alkalesi] [Wikt-shnu]
- **شكو ماكو؟** is the standard friendly greeting ("what's up"), answered with ماكو شي اليوم. [Alkalesi] [Wikt-shaku]
- **هل** exists but Alkalesi marks it as barely used. Ask by intonation. [Alkalesi]
- **Relative اللي** is invariable and only used with a **definite** head: الأستاذ اللي يدرّس عربي. With an indefinite head it simply disappears: أكو رجّال يريد يسلّم عليك. [Alkalesi]

## 5. Pronouns

آني (I, no gender split)، إنتَ، إنتي، هو، هي، إحنا، إنتو، همّه. There is **no dual and no separate feminine plural**: إنتو and همّه cover both, with gender showing on the adjective (إنتو زينين / إنتو زينات). **No copula**: آني زين = "I am well". [Alkalesi]

Suffixes: ـي / ـني، ـنا، ـك (2ms)، **ـچ (2fs: كتابچ، شلونچ)**، ـكم، ـه، ـها، ـهم. The ـچ is a gilit signature; Mosuli qeltu keeps ـك. [Alkalesi] [Wikt-shlon]

## 6. Spelling habits

- **ق said as g is written گ** in dialect writing: گال، گلت، أگدر، شگد. Keep ق for MSA-layer and loan words. This differs from Levantine and Egyptian, where ق stays ق. [Wikt-gal] [BaghdadWiki]
- **ك said as ch is written چ**: چان، چم، چذي. [Wikt-chan] [Alkalesi]
- **Interdentals ث / ذ / ظ are pronounced and kept.** Do not "colloquialise" them into ت / د / ض the way urban Egyptian and Levantine writing does. [BaghdadJewishWiki] [Albuarabi]
- **دا- is usually written as a separate دا** before the verb: دا آكل خبز، دا أحچي. A joined داكتب also circulates; pick one. (weak, native-answer thread) [HiNative]
- **"now"**: هسّة is the etymological spelling (from هالساعة), **هسّه is what people actually type**. [Wikt-hassa]
- **پ and ڤ** appear only in foreign words and often collapse to ب / ف. [BaghdadWiki]
- **No tanween, no short-vowel marks.** There is no published Iraqi CODA, so dialect spelling online is inconsistent by nature: pick your conventions and hold them across the text. [TunCODA]

## 7. MSA leaks - kill on sight

| Leak (MSA) | Iraqi | Source |

| سوف / سـ | رح- / حـ- | [Alkalesi] |
| لن | ما راح | [Albuarabi] |
| لم | ما + past | [Alkalesi] |
| ليس | مو | [Alkalesi] [Wikt-mu] |
| الذي / التي / الذين | اللي (definite heads only) | [Alkalesi] |
| هذه | هاذي / هاي | [Alkalesi] [Wikt-hay] |
| ماذا | شنو, or the proclitic شـ- | [Alkalesi] |
| لماذا / أين / كيف / متى | ليش / وين / شلون / شوكت | [Alkalesi] |
| الآن | هسّة (informal هسّه) | [Wikt-hassa] |
| جداً | كلش (intensifies adjectives) | [Wikt-kullish] |
| كثير (quantity) | هواية (nouns and adverbs) | [Wikt-hwaya] |
| فقط | بس | [Wikt-bass] |
| لا يوجد | ماكو | [Albuarabi] |
| يستطيع | أگدر / يگدر | [Alkalesi] |
| يفعل | يسوّي | [Alkalesi] |
| قام بـ + verbal noun | plain verb | style rule, see SKILL.md |

**كلش and هواية are not interchangeable**: كلش = "very" on an adjective, هواية = "a lot / many" on a noun. They can stack: أحبچ كلش هواية. [Wikt-kullish] [Wikt-hwaya]

## 8. English tech terms

Iraqi Arabic-English code-switching is documented (WhatsApp and student-conversation studies; a paper on computer, internet, and mobile-phone loanwords), and single English nouns inserted into an Arabic sentence are the most common pattern. [Turki] [Salman17]

- **Keep English nouns in Latin script** and attach Arabic clitics before them: الـ query، هالـ build.
- **English loan nouns take Arabic plurals** in attested Iraqi data (the sound feminine plural on borrowings). [Saeed10]
- **Not verified for Iraqi:** English verbs taking Iraqi verb morphology, and any سوّى + English noun pattern. Until it is sourced, write an Iraqi verb plus the English noun as its object and keep it simple.

## 9. Worked rewrites (MSA -> Baghdadi)

Grammar forms come from the sections above; ordinary content words are not individually sourced.

**1. Progressive vs habitual** (الآن, ماذا)
- MSA: ماذا تفعل الآن؟
- Iraqi: شنو دا تسوّي هسّه؟
- The bare شنو تسوّي؟ would mean "what do you do (in general)".

**2. Future + negation** (سوف, لن, يستطيع)
- MSA: لن أستطيع أن أحضر الاجتماع، وسوف أكتب التحديث بعده.
- Iraqi: ما رح أگدر أجي للاجتماع، ورح أكتب الـ update بعدين.

**3. Existential + nominal negation** (لا يوجد, ليس)
- MSA: لا يوجد أي خطأ في الـ log، لكن الـ build ليس جاهزاً.
- Iraqi: ماكو أي error بالـ log، بس الـ build مو جاهز.

**4. Question + intensifier** (لماذا, جداً, demonstrative prefix)
- MSA: لماذا هذا الـ query بطيء جداً؟
- Iraqi: ليش هالـ query كلش بطيء؟

## 10. Deliberately NOT included (unverified)

Do not generate these, they are not attested in the sources checked: the negative-copula paradigm ماني / مانت / ماهو (the table circulating for this is **Moroccan**, and Iraqi sources say the southern group has no negation pronouns); مب / موب (Gulf, no Iraqi section in the sources); ودّي as a future marker (it is volitional, "I wish", and unsourced for Baghdadi); شمعنى for ماذا; يمتى for متى; هم / بعد for أيضاً; a دا- imperative (دا روح); a distinct ـهن feminine plural suffix. [Albuarabi] [Alkalesi]
