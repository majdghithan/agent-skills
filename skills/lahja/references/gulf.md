# Gulf Arabic (خليجي) - writing guide

**Coverage:** the Gulf core (Kuwaiti anchor) with Emirati, Najdi, and Hejazi columns. Every form below is cited to the sources at the end; anything the sources didn't settle is marked and must not be generated.

**Confidence tags:** unmarked = sourced from scholarly work, corpus papers, or Wikipedia citing them. `(weak)` = sourced, but only from Wiktionary or a teaching site: **usable, just thinner evidence**, so a reviewer knows where to look first. Anything labelled **not sourced** is different: don't generate it.

**Tags:** `[K]` Kuwaiti, `[E]` Emirati, `[B]` Bahraini, `[Q]` Qatari, `[Nj]` Najdi (Riyadh / central Saudi), `[Hj]` Hejazi (Jeddah / Mecca).

## 0. Which "Gulf"? Pick one profile

Strictly, "Gulf Arabic" means Kuwait, Bahrain, Qatar, the UAE, and eastern Saudi Arabia. Najdi and Hejazi are separate dialects, even though most online "Khaleeji" text is Saudi (about 60% of the Gumar corpus). [GulfWiki] [Gumar]

- **Country known:** use that column.
- **Only "Gulf" / "Khaleeji":** use **Kuwaiti** (the best-documented core variety) and say so in one line.
- **Saudi audience:** ask or infer Riyadh (Najdi) vs Jeddah (Hejazi). Don't blend them: Hejazi shares features with Egyptian/Levantine (حـ future, فين) that Najdi lacks. [HejaziWiki]

| Feature | Kuwaiti (default) | Emirati | Najdi | Hejazi |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| "not" + noun/adjective | مو | مب (north), مش (Abu Dhabi), ما (East Coast) | مهوب / مهيب / مانيب (inflects) | مو |
| "not" + verb | ما | ما | ما | ما |
| Future | بـ / راح | بـ, older رايح | بـ, يبي / يبا | حـ |
| In progress | قاعد + verb | قاعد, يالس (weak) | قاعد | بـ / قاعد / جالس |
| "what" | شنو (weak) / شنهو | شو (weak) | وش (weak) | إيش |
| "where" | وين | وين | وين | فين |
| "now" | الحين / هالحزة | الحين | الحين | دحين |
| "you" f suffix | ـج (أبوج) | ـج (شحالج) | ـس / ـتس | ـك |

Sources: [GulfWiki] [KuwaitiWiki] [EmiratiWiki] [NajdiWiki] [HejaziWiki] [Persson] [Aljenaie08] [Binturki] [Alaslaa25] [Wikt].

## 1. Negation

- **ما + verb**, past or present, all varieties: ما ياكل، ما كان ياكل `[K]`; ما يروح `[E]`; ما أعرف `[Nj][Hj]`. [Aljenaie08] [Qafisheh70] [NajdiWiki]
- **Non-verbal negator** (nouns, adjectives, participles, prepositions): Holes gives **مب** (variants مو، مهب، هب) as the general Gulf form. [Binturki] **The per-country table in section 0 wins**: مو in a Kuwaiti text, مب in a Northern Emirates one. Treating them as interchangeable is what makes a text read as no dialect at all.
  - `[K]` **مو**: مو مدرّسة، مو رايح، مو على الطاولة. [Aljenaie08]
  - `[E]` مب in the Northern Emirates, **مش** in Abu Dhabi (هو مش مهندس), ما on the East Coast. [EmiratiWiki] [Qafisheh70]
  - `[Nj]` inflects: مهوب (he), مهيب (she), مانيب (I). [Binturki]
  - `[Hj]` مو: مو كذا. [HejaziWiki]
- **In Kuwaiti, ما (not مو) negates** أبي، عندي، في: ما أبي، ما عندي فلوس، ما في. [Aljenaie08]
- **Kuwaiti مو + verb is a warning** ("don't!"), not a plain statement: مو تاكلين الحين. [Aljenaie08]
- **Prohibition**: لا + verb: لا تروح `[K][E][Hj]`. [Aljenaie08] [HejaziWiki]
- **Existential**: ما في `[K][E][Hj]`; also ماكو / أكو `[K]`. [Aljenaie08] [KuwaitiWiki]
- **Najdi existential is not sourced here.** For "there is no X" in a Riyadh text, restructure rather than guess: negate the predicate with the مهوب family (حسابك مهوب فيه مشكلة) or rebuild the sentence. Don't import ماكو, which is Kuwaiti and Iraqi.
- **No Egyptian-style ما...ش** anywhere in Gulf, Najdi, or Hejazi. Never write ما أعرفش. [HejaziWiki] [Binturki]

## 2. Future

- **بـ + verb** = future or intention (Kuwaiti, Bahraini, Qatari, Emirati, Najdi): باروح = "I'm going to go". It contrasts with أبي أروح = "I'd like to go". [Persson]
- **راح + verb** `[K][B]`, also some Saudi speakers; more common among younger educated speakers. [Gumar] [Persson]
- **حـ** is Hejazi only: حيدرس. Not core Gulf. [HejaziWiki]
- **Negated future**: ما راح ياكل `[K]` (never ~~راح ما ياكل~~); ما بـ: ما بيعرفون. [Aljenaie08] [Persson]

## 3. Aspect and mood

- **قاعد + verb = in progress**, across Najd, Kuwait, Bahrain, Qatar, UAE: قاعدين يطالعون فلم. Past progressive: كان قاعد. [NajdiWiki] [KuwaitiWiki]
- **Bare imperfect = habitual or present**: يتكلم = speaks / is speaking. **Don't import the Levantine/Egyptian بـ present**: in core Gulf, بـ marks future/intent (irrealis), so بيروح reads as "will go". [Qafisheh70] [Persson]
- `[Hj]` is different: بيدرس = is studying. [HejaziWiki]
- **After أبي / لازم / أقدر: bare verb**, no أنْ: أبي أروح، لازم تكونين، ما أقدر أمشي. [Persson] [Qafisheh70]

## 4. Demonstratives and question words

- **this / these**: هذا، هذي، هذيل `[E]`; that: ذاك. `[Nj]` ذا / ذي / ذولا (weak). [Qafisheh70] [Binturki] [Wikt]
- **Question words**: شنو / شنهو / وش / إيش (by variety, see table), ليش (all), شلون or كيف (both Gulf), وين (Hejazi فين), منو `[K]` (weak), من، متى، كم. [GulfWiki] [HejaziWiki] [Wikt]
- **Relative**: اللي. [NajdiWiki] [Qafisheh70]

## 5. Pronouns

أنا، إحنا (`[Q]` also حنا)، إنت، إنتِ، إنتو / إنتم، هو، هي، هم. Many speakers merge plural genders. [GulfWiki]

Suffixes: masculine 2sg **ـك** (ملفك، عليك), plural **ـكم**, 3ms **ـه**, 3fs **ـها**, 3pl **ـهم**, as elsewhere in Arabic. [Qafisheh70]

**Feminine "you" suffix is the big tell:**
- Core Gulf: **ـج** (pronounced ch): أبوج، شلونج، شحالج. [GulfWiki] [Gumar]
- Najdi: ـس or ـتس (kaskasa). Southern/Eastern Province: ـش. [Alaslaa25]
- Hejazi keeps ـك. [HejaziWiki]

**Time words are not covered here.** Beyond "now" (الحين), this guide has no sourced Gulf words for tomorrow, today, or later. بكرة is pan-Arabic and safe; don't reach for a local form you can't source.

## 6. Spelling habits

- **ق stays ق** in writing (said g, or dʒ near front vowels). Gumar/CODA keeps the letter. گ appears in some Kuwaiti loanwords. [GulfWiki] [Gumar] [KuwaitiWiki]
- **چ** is used for the ch sound, often typed as ج for lack of a key: شلونچ / شلونج. Pick one per text. (weak) [Wikt] [Gumar]
- **ج said as y** (ريّال، يالس) is optional in speech and never happens in recent MSA borrowings. Spelling it ي exists (weak); the CODA convention keeps ج. **Default: keep ج** unless the brand writes phonetically. [GulfWiki] [Gumar]
- **Interdentals ث / ذ / ظ are pronounced**, so keep them: هذا، ثلاثة. (Hejazi merges them.) [GulfWiki] [HejaziWiki]
- **Final ة**: write ة. [Gumar]
- **No tanween, no short-vowel marks** in informal text; shadda optional (see SKILL.md).

## 8. English tech terms

- **Arabic-English mixing is ordinary** in the Gulf, though still frowned on in formal settings. [Hopkyns22]
- **Arabized verbs are attested**: كنسل (بكنسل حجزي), چيّك `[Gulf]` / شيّك `[Hj]`. General colloquial Arabic, per a Saudi-based study: شيّك، سيّف، شيّر، فرمت، هنّج، هكّر. [Wikt] [AlJarf24] [HejaziWiki]
- **Some English verbs stay unadapted** with a light verb "do" (ابديت، كومنت، منشن). [AlJarf24]
- **Not verified for Gulf:** سوّى + English noun ("سويت deploy"), and whether professionals write English terms in Latin script inline. Until someone sources them, follow the lahja SKILL.md defaults (Latin script for names, code, and acronyms) and use Arabized verbs only from the list above.

## 9. Worked rewrites (MSA -> Kuwaiti)

These rewrites use the grammar forms above; common content words (كمّل، جاهز، اجتماع) are not individually sourced.

**1. Want / can't** (يريد, يستطيع, لكن)
- MSA: لا أريد أن ألغي المشروع، لكنني لا أستطيع أن أكمل الآن.
- Kuwaiti: ما أبي أكنسل المشروع، بس ما أقدر أكمّل الحين.

**2. Questions** (ماذا, progressive, existential)
- MSA: ماذا تفعل الآن؟ ولماذا لا يوجد update؟
- Kuwaiti: شنو قاعد تسوّي الحين؟ وليش ما في update؟

**3. Nominal negation + negated future** (ليس, لن)
- MSA: الكود ليس جاهزاً، ولن نحضر الاجتماع اليوم.
- Kuwaiti: الكود مو جاهز، وما راح نحضر الاجتماع اليوم.

**4. Same line, Northern Emirati** (مب, feminine ـج)
- MSA: كيف حالكِ؟ الملف ليس في الـ folder.
- Emirati: شحالج؟ الملف مب في الـ folder.
- Abu Dhabi: swap مب for مش.
