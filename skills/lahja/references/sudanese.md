# Sudanese Arabic (سوداني) - writing guide

**Coverage:** Khartoum / central Sudanese, the media standard. Rural forms are marked where the source marks them. Every form below is cited to the sources at the end; anything the sources didn't settle is marked and must not be generated.

**Main source:** James Dickins, "Khartoum Arabic" (Encyclopedia of Arabic Language and Linguistics), the only full academic grammar sketch available in full text. Cited as [Dickins].

## 0. Sudanese is not Egyptian. Four traps

An agent that defaults to Egyptian gets all four of these wrong:

| Point | Egyptian | Sudanese |
|---|---|---|
| "there isn't" | مفيش | **ما في**. Sudanese **ما فيش** is something else: an adjective meaning "absent, not here", and it **inflects** (ما فيشة، ما فيشين). [Dickins] |
| "the one who / which" | اللي | **الـ** attached straight to the verb: القال كدي منو؟ = "who said that". [Dickins] |
| "these" | دول | **ديل** [Dickins] |
| 1sg past | دَرِست | **درستَ**, identical in form to 2ms, with a doubled final t. [Dickins] [AlRawafi20] |

Plus: ج is a palatal [ɟ] (jamīl, never Cairene gamīl), which is the loudest audible Sudanese marker. [SudanWiki]

## 1. Negation

- **ما + predicate is the normal negator, and there is no ...ش suffix**: إنت ما بتفهم عربي، ما صدقتَ. It works the same before a noun, adjective, or prepositional phrase: إنت ما راجل، ما كويس، ما في البيت. [Dickins]
- **Existential**: ما في: ما في زول يمشي معاي. [Dickins]
- **ما فيش is NOT the existential.** It is an adjective, "absent / not there", inflecting ما فيش / ما فيشة / ما فيشين. Using it the Egyptian way is a straight error. [Dickins]
- **Negative copula = ما + pronoun**, again with no ش: ما أنا، ما أنت، ما أنتي، ماهو، ماهي. Sudanese patterns with Kuwaiti here, against Jordanian مانيش. [AlRawafi20]
- **مش is marked as rare** in Sudanese. Treat it as a Cairo leak and avoid it. [AlRawafi20]
- **Negative imperative = ما + imperfect** (not لا + jussive): ما تصدم الراجل. [AlRawafi20]
- **Possession**: ما في معاك = "you don't have". [AlRawafi20]
- **Not sourced:** the plural negative copula forms (ما إحنا / ما إنتو / ما هم). The source table leaves them blank.

## 2. Future

- **حـ + imperfect** is the dedicated future: حأجيكم بكرة. It is a 20th-century borrowing from Egyptian, now fully established in Khartoum. [Dickins]
- **بـ + imperfect also carries the future**: باجيكم بكرة, glossed the same way in the same source. [Dickins]
- **راح is not Sudanese** in the sources checked; it is assigned to Kuwaiti and Jordanian. Don't use it. [AlRawafi20]
- **Not sourced:** ناوي as a future marker.

## 3. Aspect

- **بـ- is a real indicative prefix in Sudanese** (unlike Gulf, where بـ is future). Three uses: [Dickins]
  - happening now: بتسوي شنو؟
  - habitual: بقوم الساعة ستة
  - generic: الشغل ما بينتهي
  Corroborated by native-speaker data: أنا بحب رمضان. [ArSyra]
- **قاعد + imperfect is the emphatic progressive**, stronger and more deliberate than بـ: قاعد تسوي شنو؟ It inflects: قاعد / قاعدة / قاعدين / قاعدات. [Dickins]
- **Bare imperfect** after modals and in purpose clauses: أنا جيت أشوف شغل، ما بتقدر تشوف، تدفع بعدين. [Dickins]
- **"Want" is داير** (inflects: داير / دايرة / دايرين): داير الشيء ده. **عاوز is also genuine Sudanese** (Dickins' own example is أنا عاوز نشتغل), so don't teach داير as the only form, just the distinctive one. [Mo3jam] [ArWiki] [Dickins]
- **Not sourced:** لازم + bare verb in Sudanese specifically.

## 4. Demonstratives and question words

- **Near: ده (m) / دي (f) / ديل (pl)**. **Far: داك / ديك / ديلاك**. [Dickins] [ArWiki]
- **Question words**: شنو (what)، منو (who)، وين (where)، كيف (how)، متين (when)، ياتو (which). [Dickins]
- **ياتو has an agreement rule** worth getting right: invariable before the noun (ياتو راجل، ياتو مرة، ياتو أولاد), inflected after it (الراجل ياتو؟ الم
رة ياتي؟ الأولاد ياتم؟). [Dickins]
- **كيف and وين take pronoun suffixes**: كيفك / كيفِك is the everyday "how are you". [Dickins]
- **Question words stay in place**: ماشي وين؟ is neutral; وين ماشي؟ is emphatic. Fronting by default is an Egyptian habit, not Sudanese. [Dickins]
- **Relative = الـ, not اللي.** It attaches directly to verbs, adjectives, adverbs, even whole clauses: الزعل ("the one who got angry")، الراجل الزعلتَ منو، القال كدي منو؟، الهسي، الأنا زعلتَ منو. The source never mentions اللي at all. [Dickins]
- **Not sourced:** any word for "why" (neither ليه nor مالك could be confirmed), and كم / كام. Restructure instead of guessing.

## 5. Pronouns

نحنا (written إحنا too; Dickins transliterates niḥna)، أنا، إنت، إنتي، إنتو، هو، هي، هم. Feminine plurals (هن) are marked rural, not Khartoum standard. [Dickins]

Suffixes: ـي / ـني، ـك (2ms)، ـِك / ـكي (2fs)، ـو / ـهو (3ms)، ـها (3fs)، ـنا، **ـكم** (2pl)، ـهم (3pl). [Dickins]

- **The 1sg perfect ends in ـتَ**, the same shape as 2ms, with a doubled final t: درستَ. An agent defaulting to Egyptian دَرِست gets every past-tense "I" wrong. [Dickins] [AlRawafi20]
- **Not sourced:** a ـكو 2pl suffix. The source prints ـكم.

## 6. Spelling habits

- **ق is pronounced g but stays ق in writing.** Don't respell it گ or ج. [SudanWiki] [ArWiki]
- **ج stays ج**, pronounced as a palatal [ɟ]: جميل. [SudanWiki]
- **ث and ذ shift to ت and د** in inherited words, and the source's phoneme inventory has no interdentals: كتير. This is the opposite of Gulf and Iraqi, which keep them. [SudanWiki] [Dickins]
- **Write in Arabic script.** The large Sudanese social-media corpora are Arabic script; Sudanese Arabizi exists (Samh = سمح، kityer = كتير) but is the minority channel. [SudSenti] [Mhamed22]
- **Not sourced:** an explicit convention for final ة or tanween. Observed practice keeps ة and drops tanween; follow the general lahja spelling rules in SKILL.md.

## 7. MSA leaks - kill on sight

| Leak (MSA) | Sudanese | Source |
|---|---|---|
| سوف / سـ | حـ (also بـ) | [Dickins] |
| لم / لن / ليس | ما + predicate | [Dickins] |
| ليس (copula) | ماهو / ماهي / ما أنا | [AlRawafi20] |
| الذي / التي | **الـ** attached to the verb, not اللي | [Dickins] |
| هذا / هذه / هؤلاء | ده / دي / ديل | [Dickins] |
| ماذا | شنو | [Dickins] [SudLLM] |
| أين / كيف / متى | وين / كيف / متين | [Dickins] |
| الآن | هسع (also هسي); "right now" = هسع دا | [Dickins] [ArWiki] |
| جداً | خالص، جداً، جد (جد goes with adjectives like صعب، زعلان) | [Dickins] |
| كثير | كتير | [Dickins] |
| يريد | داير (also عاوز) | [Mo3jam] [Dickins] |
| يستطيع | يقدر / بتقدر | [Dickins] |
| لا يوجد | ما في | [Dickins] |
| يفعل | سوّى (بتسوي، تسوي) | [Dickins] |
| هكذا | كدي / كده | [Dickins] |
| ملكي | بتاعي / حقي (both inflect: بتاعة، بتاعين، حقة، حقين) | [Dickins] [ArWiki] |
| قام بـ + verbal noun | plain verb | style rule, see SKILL.md |

- **Inalienable things take the plain genitive**: رجلي "my leg", never الرجل بتاعتي. [Dickins]
- **شديد for "very"** appears only in popular sources, not in the grammar. Use خالص as the default and treat شديد as colloquial colour. [IndustryArabic]
- **Not sourced for Sudanese:** فقط -> بس, and أيضاً -> كمان. Both are plausible and neither is confirmed, so restructure rather than assert them.

## 8. English tech terms

- **Arabic-English code-switching among educated Sudanese is documented**, though the published work is classroom-focused rather than professional register. [JLTR] [CSSurvey]
- **Borrow nouns, not verbs.** No source documents Sudanese loan verbs with Arabic morphology (no يسيّف / نكنسل equivalents), so don't invent them. Keep the English noun in Latin script and use a Sudanese verb with it: بسوي deploy، ما بقدر أفتح الـ file.
- **Arabic script is the default** for Sudanese online writing. [SudSenti] [Mhamed22]

## 9. Worked rewrites (MSA -> Khartoum)

Grammar comes from the sections above; ordinary content words are not individually sourced.

**1. What are you doing now** (progressive, question in place, الآن)
- MSA: ماذا تفعل الآن؟
- Sudanese: بتسوي شنو هسع؟
- Question word stays at the end; بـ carries "right now".

**2. Can't come** (لن, يستطيع, غداً)
- MSA: لن أستطيع أن آتي غداً، وسوف أكتب التحديث.
- Sudanese: ما بقدر أجي بكرة، وحأكتب الـ update.

**3. Existential + relative** (لا يوجد, الذي, من)
- MSA: من قال هذا؟ لا يوجد أحد هنا.
- Sudanese: منو القال كدي؟ ما في زول هنا.
- الـ does the relative work; اللي would read Egyptian.

**4. Want + these** (أريد, هؤلاء, ليس)
- MSA: أريد هذه الملفات، وهي ليست جاهزة.
- Sudanese: داير الملفات ديل، وهي ما جاهزة.

## 10. Deliberately NOT included

Not attested in the sources checked, so don't generate them: a "why" word (ليه or مالك); كم / كام; فقط -> بس; أيضاً -> كمان; راح as a future marker; ناوي; a ـكو 2pl suffix; plural negative copulas (ما إحنا / ما إنتو / ما هم); Sudanese loan verbs from English; لازم + bare verb. Two web sources were checked and **rejected** as wrong: one claiming Sudanese has no بـ prefix and keeps the interdentals, and one applying MSA ليس / لا rules to the dialect.
