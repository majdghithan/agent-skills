# Yemeni Arabic (يمني) - writing guide

**Coverage:** Sanaani (صنعاني), the northern highland variety. It is the largest (about 12.6M speakers), the best documented, and the one heard outside its home region. Notes for Ta'izzi-Adeni, Hadhrami, Tihami, and Yafi'i where sourced. Every form below is cited to the sources at the end; anything the sources didn't settle is listed in the last section and must not be generated.

**Main source:** Qafisheh, *Yemeni Arabic I and II* (University of Arizona), full text online, cited as [Qafisheh]. Watson's grammars are paywalled, so anything from Watson comes through sources that quote her.

## 0. Yemen is the most fragmented dialect area in this skill

Sanaani, Hadhrami, Ta'izzi-Adeni, and Tihami differ sharply, and Yafi'i, Adini, Ibbi and others differ again. **Default to Sanaani** and say so; switch only when the user names a region. [YemenWiki] [ArWikiYemen]

| Feature | Sanaani (default) | Ta'izzi-Adeni | Hadhrami | Yafi'i |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| "want" | أشتي | أشتي | بغيت | أبي |
| Future | شـ / شا‍ـ (1sg strongest), عـ other persons | شـ (Adini group) | - | آ‍ـ (آروح) |
| ق | [g] | [q] | [g] | [ʁ] |
| ج | [dʒ] | [g] | [j] coastal | [g] |
| Past 1sg/2sg marker | ـت (عملت) | **ـك** (عملك، ما عملكش) | - | **ـك** (رحك، أكلك) |
| "where" | أين / من أين | فين | - | وين |

## 1. Negation

- **ما + verb + ـش** is the Sanaani verbal negation, past and present: ما يشربش بيرة، ما تدرسش، ما نشتيش، ما سرتش، ما صبحش. [Qafisheh]
- **The ـش is optional but usual**, so ما يشرب is also grammatical. The final vowel lengthens before it: ما تشتيش، ما يروحوش، ما يجيش. [Qafisheh]
- **Nominal negation = ماش / ماشي**, not مش and not مو: هو ماش مهندس، الجو ماش بارد، هي ماش هنا، أنا ماش داري ("I don't know"). [Qafisheh] [Alqahtani20]
- **مو is Gulf, not Yemeni.** It appears in no Yemeni row of the sources checked.
- **Existential**: positive **به** or **في**; negative **مابش / ما بهش**: مابش ماء، مابش فرق. Past: كان به / ما كانش به. "Nobody" is **محّد**: محّد جاء. [Qafisheh]
- **Prohibition in Sanaani = لا + imperfect, with NO ـش**: لا تجي، لا تشرب بيرة، لا تتزوج ذلحين. (Southern Ibbi does use لا ت...ش: لا تمنعش الكلام. Don't mix the two.) [Qafisheh] [Shormani]
- **Pseudo-verbs take the same circumfix**: ما عندكش جهال؟، ما عندناش قات، ما يقدرش يتزوج. [Qafisheh]
- **غير negates adjectives**, distinct from ماش: الخط غير ناهي، غير طويل وغير قصير. [Qafisheh]

## 2. Future

- **شـ / شا‍ـ is the Sanaani future**: شاعمل، شارجع. Written with ا, not أ. In Sanaa city it is strongest in the 1sg; the surrounding villages use it for all persons. [Qafisheh] [SanaaWiki] [ArWikiSanaa]
- **عـ / عا‍ـ covers the other persons** in Sanaa proper. [Qafisheh] [SanaaWiki]
- **بـ can carry the future** when the context is temporal: بيتكلم غدوة؟ [Qafisheh]
- Regional: Tihami uses شـ for all persons (شابوك امسوق); Yafi'i uses آ‍ـ (آروح). [TihamiWiki] [YafiiWiki]
- **Not sourced for any Yemeni variety:** راح, حـ, رح. Don't use them.

## 3. Aspect

- **Sanaani has the بـ indicative prefix**, and it is the **progressive**, not the habitual: [Qafisheh]
  - bare imperfect = habitual or generic: أتكلم عربي كل يوم
  - بـ + imperfect = happening now: هو بيتكلم عربي ذلحين، أيش بتعمل هنا؟، أنا ما بأدرسش ذلحين
- **عاد means "still / yet / remaining"**, not future: عاد به = "there is still some", وعاد أشتي أقول = "and I still want to say". [ArWikiSanaa] [Qafisheh]
- **قد marks the perfective or "already"**, negated ما قد ... ش: ما قد مرضش من أول. [Qafisheh]
- **Modals take the bare imperfect**, no linker and no بـ: أشتي أسافر، أشتي أكلمك وأروح، ما يقدرش يتزوج، أيش تشتي تشرب؟ [Qafisheh]
- **"Want" is أشتي**, the distinctly Yemeni verb: أشتي / تشتي / يشتي / نشتي / تشتوا / يشتوا, negated ما يشتيش. أبغى exists in Sanaani but is far rarer. [Qafisheh] [ArWikiSanaa]

## 4. Demonstratives and question words

- **Near**: هاذا / هاذي / هاذولاء. **Far**: هذاك / هذيك / هاذولاك. The bare ذيّا / ذيّه / ذولائي series exists but is explicitly less common, so default to هاذا. [Qafisheh]
- Word order changes the meaning: هاذي البنت حالية = "this girl is beautiful" (a sentence); هاذي بنت حالية = "this is a beautiful girl". [Qafisheh]
- **Question words**: [Qafisheh] [ArWikiSanaa]

| Meaning | Sanaani |
|---|---|
| what | **أيش**; classical **ما** survives in fixed questions: ما اسمك؟، ما شغلك؟، ما لون هاذا؟ |
| who | من (من أنت؟) |
| where | أين / من أين (southern فين، Yafi'i وين) |
| why | **للمه** (south لمه / لمو / ليش) |
| how | كيف (كيف أنت؟، كيف حالش؟) |
| when | متى |
| how much | كم (كم عمرك؟) |
| which | أيّ |

- **Relative**: Sanaani keeps **اللذي**, invariable, used for feminines and plurals too: هاذولا البنات اللذي أبصرتهن، هو اللذي جئت معاه. **اللي** is a listed variant and is safe; اللذي is the marked Sanaani form. [Qafisheh]
- **Sanaani keeps the ما أفعل exclamative** (ما أجمل), which most dialects have lost. [SanaaWiki]

## 5. Pronouns

أنا، إحنا، أنت (m) / أنتِ (f)، أنتو، هو، هي، هم / هن. [SanaaWiki]

- **The 2fs suffix is ـش**, the loudest northern Yemeni marker: ما اسمش؟، أين شغلش؟، كيف حالش؟، زوجش. 2ms stays ـك. [SanaaWiki] [Qafisheh] [Alqahtani20]
- **چ is Gulf and Iraqi, never Yemeni.**
- **Politeness: Sanaani addresses parents, elders, and strangers in the plural**: كيف حالكم يا أباه؟، تشربوا شيء يا عم؟ Worth respecting in any customer-facing text. [ArWikiSanaa]
- Regional: Hadhrami splits the 1sg by gender (أنا m / أني f); Adeni and Yafi'i mark the past with ـك (عملك، ما عملكش) where Sanaani uses ـت. [YemenWiki] [ArWikiYemen]

## 6. Spelling habits

- **ق is [g] in Sanaani but stays ق in writing**: قوّيت، يقدر، قد، قات. Don't respell it گ or ج. [ArWikiSanaa] [YemenWiki]
- **ج is [dʒ] in Sanaani** (it is [g] in Tihami, Ta'izzi-Adeni, and Yafi'i, and [j] on the Hadhrami coast). Script stays ج everywhere. [SanaaWiki] [YemenWiki]
- **Interdentals ث ذ ظ are retained.** This is the headline conservative feature: write ثلاثة، هاذا، ذلحين, never تلاتة / هادا. [SanaaWiki] [TaizziWiki]
- **ض merges with ظ** in Sanaani. [ArWikiSanaa]
- **No tanween in Sanaani.** Dialectal tanween is real in Arabic, but it belongs to the western Tihama belt, not to the anchor variety, and the Sanaani course texts contain none. MSA adverbial tanween (جداً، أيضاً) is a leak and should go. [AlJallad] [RijalAlma] [Qafisheh]
- **Final ة** is written normally. [Qafisheh]
- **Not sourced:** any Yemen-specific Arabizi convention. Default to Arabic script.

## 8. English tech terms

- **Established loanwords exist in Sanaani lexis** (galaṣ "glass", edarapp "to drop", bardag, saykal), and edarapp is a genuine loan verb with Arabic morphology. So borrowing is normal in principle. [SanaaWiki citing Piamenta]
- **No study of Yemeni tech register, code-switching, or online script practice could be found.** So this guide takes the conservative route: **write the tech term in Arabic script** inside an otherwise Arabic sentence, as the loanword evidence suggests, and **don't fabricate Yemeni-specific tech slang or loan verbs**. If the user's own text uses Latin-script terms, follow their lead.
- Sanaa is one of the 25 cities in the MADAR corpus, so the variety does have machine-readable representation if someone wants to go deeper. [MADAR]

## 9. Worked rewrites (MSA -> Sanaani)

Grammar comes from the sections above; ordinary content words are not individually sourced.

**1. Want + can't** (يريد, يستطيع, the ما...ش circumfix)
- MSA: أريد أن أسافر، لكنني لا أستطيع الآن.
- Sanaani: أشتي أسافر، بس ما أقدرش ذلحين.

**2. Progressive + what** (بـ as progressive, أيش, الآن)
- MSA: ماذا تفعل الآن؟
- Sanaani: أيش بتعمل ذلحين؟

**3. Nominal negation + existential** (ليس, لا يوجد)
- MSA: الملف ليس جاهزاً، ولا يوجد فرق.
- Sanaani: الملف ماش جاهز، ومابش فرق.

**4. Future + addressing a woman** (سوف, the ـش 2fs suffix)
- MSA: سوف أرسل لكِ التقرير غداً.
- Sanaani: شاعمل لش التقرير غدوة.
- For an elder or a customer, Sanaani would use the plural: شاعمل لكم التقرير غدوة.

## 10. Deliberately NOT included

Not attested in the sources checked, so don't generate them: **كيش** for "how" (crowd dictionary only; use كيف); **وين** for Sanaani specifically (it is verified for Yafi'i and general Yemen, not for the anchor); **منو**; **شو** (Levantine); **ماذا** and **مو**; **راح / حـ / رح** as futures; **بزاف** (Maghrebi); **سوّى** for "to do"; **چ** as a 2fs suffix; **tanween in Sanaani**; a replacement for أيضاً (none was found: rephrase rather than borrowing the Levantine كمان); and any Yemen-specific Arabizi or code-switching rule.
